2) Transformations of sine/cosine/exponential

Given y = A·f(bx) + c, report amplitude/period/axis for circular functions and asymptote/scale/shift for exponentials.

Hint
For y=A·sin(bx)+c: amplitude=|A|, period=2π/|b|, midline y=c. For y=A·cos(bx)+c same rules. For y=A·abx+c (a>0≠1): horizontal asymptote y=c, growth if a>1 and A>0 (otherwise reflections).

4) Exponentials & logarithms — inverses and features

Hint
If y = A·abx+c then the inverse (when A,b≠0) has form x = (1/b)·loga((y−c)/A). Horizontal asymptote at y=c. Log graphs have vertical asymptote at x = −c/A only after horizontal shifts on input; base a>1 increases.
Solutions

5) Limits & central difference derivative approximation

Hint
Central difference at x with step h: f′(x) ≈ [f(x+h) − f(x−h)]/(2h). Error is O(h²) for smooth f.
Solutions

6) Newton’s method for a cubic

Hint
Newton iteration: xn+1 = xn − f(xn)/f′(xn). Stop when |change| is tiny.

7) Polynomial calculus — derivative, antiderivative & optimisation

Hint
Power rule: (xⁿ)′ = n xⁿ⁻¹; ∫ xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1) + C for n≠−1. Critical points where f′=0; classify via sign change of f′ or f″.

8) Probability — compound, independence, conditional & law of total probability

Hint
Addition rule: P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B). Independent ⇒ P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B). Conditional: P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B). Law of total probability: if {Bᵢ} partitions Ω then P(A)=Σ P(A|Bᵢ)P(Bᵢ).
